MightyMonitor for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) - early test version

Show your screen on another device, or watch another device's screen on this PC, over your own home or office Wi-Fi. It is free, needs no account, and stays on your network.

What Setup does
- Installs MightyMonitor for your Windows account only. No administrator rights needed.
- The first time, it downloads some video support. You need internet for this, and some extra disk space.

What Setup does not do
- It does not start sharing. Sharing starts only when you press "Share this screen" in the app.
- It does not change your firewall, install display drivers or let anyone control this PC.

First use
- Windows may say it "protected your PC" because this early version is not signed yet. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
- The first time you share, Windows may ask to allow network access. Choose "Allow" so your other device can connect.
- Open MightyMonitor from the Start menu. Step-by-step help: https://github.com/reduckyLang/MightyMonitor/blob/main/docs/GETTING_STARTED.md

To remove it
- Open Windows Settings, then Apps, and uninstall MightyMonitor.
- The video support is a separate program and stays, so other programs can keep using it. You can remove it from the same Apps list if you no longer need it.
